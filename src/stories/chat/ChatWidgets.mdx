# Chat widgets

The visual answers the chat homepage shows under a reply. A visitor who picks a starter question gets a short, pre-built tour: two or three lines of text, each with at most one widget. A visitor who types a question gets an answer from the AI model, which can attach up to two of these same widgets.

Widgets do the showing so the text can stay short. Every fact in them comes from the case study content in `src/content/`. A widget never adds a claim of its own.

## How a widget reaches the screen

- **From a tour.** `src/chat/topics.ts` lists widget ids on each beat. No model call is involved.
- **From the model.** A reply ending in `[[show:sabre.outcomes]]` gets that widget. The client keeps only ids listed in `src/chat/widgetIds.ts`, at most two per reply, so an invented id renders nothing.
- Either way, `ChatWidget` turns the id into UI. That is the only place it happens.

## Hidden until there's content

- **Gallery** (`<study>.gallery`) shows real screenshots from `src/content/work-images.ts` and renders nothing until an image has a `src`. Sagent never gets one, because its work is under NDA.
- **Side projects** (`side-projects`) render nothing until `src/content/side-projects.ts` has an entry. Its starter question stays hidden until then too.

## Do's

- **Add new widgets to the registry.** That means a component, an id in `widgetIds.ts`, a case in `ChatWidget`, and, if the model should be able to use it, a line in the WIDGETS section of `api/lib/system-prompt.ts`.
- **Change the content file, not the widget.** Copy lives in `src/content/`.
- **Keep labels at 13px or larger.** This is the floor set by the readability review (`docs/case-study/chat-widget-review.md`).

## Don'ts

- **Don't show placeholder frames.** In a conversation, an empty frame reads as a mistake.
- **Don't put jargon in sketches.** If a label needs explaining, the sketch isn't doing its job.
- **Don't let the model narrate a widget.** "Linked below" and "see the card" are forbidden in the brief.

## AI guidance

Render chat widgets only through `ChatWidget`, with an id from `src/chat/widgetIds.ts`. Never build a one-off card in the transcript.

**Content rules**

- Widget copy comes from `src/content/`. Case study text inside widgets stays in Ben's first person, and the chat lines around them stay in third person.
- Tours use two or three beats, each with one main widget. The full case study is opt-in.
- The model may use at most two widgets per reply, and only ids the system prompt lists.
