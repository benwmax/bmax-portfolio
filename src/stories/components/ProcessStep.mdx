## Writing the body

The body is the hardest part to get right. It should read as an observation, not a framework recitation.

<Canvas of={ProcessStories.FieldResearch} />

| Instead of... | Write... |
|---------------|----------|
| "Conducted field observation sessions with agents" | "They kept leaving the tool mid-call for Expedia" |
| "Facilitated a cross-functional design sprint" | "Sprint to surface competing stakeholder priorities and get testable directions on the table fast" |
| "Performed heuristic analysis" | "Heuristic read on what was actually broken — no redesigning before we knew what was wrong" |

## Phase labels

One word: `Assess`, `Align`, `Build`, `Discover`, `Define`, `Test`

## Artifact format

Method names joined with `·`: `Field observation · Analytics review`, `A/B testing · Service blueprint`

## Do's

- **Wrap steps in `ProcessSteps`.** Never lay out cards manually.
- **Write body as what happened.** Past tense, first or third person.
- **1–3 sentences in the body.** One sharp observation beats a bullet of frameworks.

## Don'ts

- **Don't exceed 5 steps.** The process section needs to be scannable.
- **Don't write "Conducted X methodology."** Describe the outcome or finding instead.
- **Don't use ProcessSteps outside of a case study process section.**

## AI guidance

Numbered process step cards for case study process sections. Always use the `ProcessSteps` wrapper for multiple steps. Body reads like something that happened — not a methodology list.

**Content rules**
- `num`: 1-based integer.
- `phase`: one-word label — `Assess`, `Align`, `Build`, `Discover`, `Define`, `Test`.
- `title`: one-line step label.
- `body`: 1–3 sentences describing what actually happened. First person or observational — "They kept leaving the tool mid-call for Expedia" not "Conducted field observation sessions."
- `artifact`: method names joined with `·` — `"Field observation · Analytics review"`, `"A/B testing · Service blueprint"`.
- Always wrap steps in `ProcessSteps` — never lay out cards manually.

**Avoid**
- Don't write body as a methodology recitation: `"Conducted X methodology"`. Write what happened.
- Don't exceed 5 steps — the process section should be scannable.
- Don't use `ProcessSteps` outside of a case study process section.
