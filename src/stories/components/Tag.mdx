# Tag

Informational label — Space Mono, ultra-wide tracking. Two color families: amber for industry/context, green for method/process. Tags are **never interactive** — no `onClick`, no `href`.

## Variants

### Amber (default) — industry labels

Used in case study card footers and hero meta blocks.

<Canvas of={TagStories.Default} />

### Green — method/process labels

Reads as "this is what we did," not "this is what it is."

<Canvas of={TagStories.Green} />

### Solid — high-emphasis only

Reserved for callout contexts where a bordered tag would get lost. Not used in card layouts.

<Canvas of={TagStories.Solid} />

## Do's

- **Use amber for industry.** Travel, Fintech, Mortgage, Insurance, AI Collaboration.
- **Use green for method.** Design Sprint, Research, Field Study, AI Collaboration (when method-framed).
- **Singular, title-case.** "Fintech" not "Fintechs" or "FINTECH".

## Don'ts

- **Don't make Tags interactive.** They carry no `onClick`, `href`, or focus state.
- **Don't invent new industry labels.** Only the five canonical labels are used.
- **Don't use amber for interactive contexts.** Amber means "context," not "action."
