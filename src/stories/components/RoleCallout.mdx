# RoleCallout

Explicit ownership rows for the Role section of a case study — 132px fixed label column in mono-ui uppercase, flowing prose content column in sans serif. The label/content contrast creates hierarchy without headings: labels read as a spec sheet, content reads as a story.

Always use the `RoleCallouts` wrapper for multiple rows.

## Standard 3-row pattern

What was owned, what the director layer handled, what the key in-the-room moment was.

## Label vocabulary

| Label | When to use |
|-------|-------------|
| `Owned` | The scope of direct ownership |
| `Director layer` | What was handled above the player |
| `In the room` | The key moment or highest-stakes contribution |
| `Started as` | Opening scope (Sagent pattern only) |
| `Became` | Expanded scope after an unexpected event |

## Do's

- **Wrap rows in `RoleCallouts`.** Never lay out rows manually.
- **Write content in first person, active voice.** "I sat between the team and our Director."
- **Be specific.** "I ran stakeholder alignment for 12 teams" beats "I was the lead designer."
- **Limit to 3 rows.** Pick the three that define scope and level.

## Don'ts

- **Don't list every task.** One row per ownership dimension, not one per responsibility.
- **Don't use the "Started as / Became" pattern for stable roles.** It's specific to unexpected scope expansion.
